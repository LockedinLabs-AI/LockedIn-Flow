# Code of conduct

LockedIn Labs welcomes useful, respectful participation in this project.

- Be considerate and constructive. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Make room for different backgrounds and experience levels.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, or publish private information.
- Keep all reports and examples free of credentials, customer information,
  personal data, and protected health information.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, or restrict
participation when needed to keep the project safe and useful. To report a
conduct concern privately, use the [LockedIn Labs contact form](https://lockedinlabs.ai/contact/)
to request a private channel.
